Design notes

  • Order: Stats (small, for motivation), then Needs you (the focal point, pink edge is the only pink in a list), then every Application grouped by Status. Concept "B on top of A".
  • Needs you kinds: follow-up due, thank-you due, email to match, upcoming interview, offer to answer. At most one primary per item. Items only coming up get one quiet "Coming up" line.
  • Response rate counts a rejection as a reply and leaves out Applications under 14 days old, and says how many are left out.
  • Weekly chart: one series, pine. The current week is a pale bar labelled "so far". The bar turns pink only when the current week beats the median pace, and the label says so. Every bar has a tooltip and the numbers are in a table.
  • Quick status change is a labelled select per row. A change makes an Event "by you" and shows a 10-second undo toast. Accepted asks about withdrawing the others.
  • Nothing sends: every draft is copied or opened in Mail by the user, then "Mark sent".
