Design system

Calm and encouraging for a search that involves rejection. Mountain stone and pine, soft surfaces, and one alpine-pink accent that only ever means "needs you" or "something went well".

Pink is a signal, not a colour

Alpine pink appears only for things that need you and for wins (an offer, a streak). Everywhere else, pine and stone do the work.

Progress is an ascent

Momentum is drawn as a climbing line, because volume and consistency are what you control when replies are out of your hands.

Calm density

Tables stay information-dense at a 15px body; air goes between groups, not inside rows. Soft surfaces, never fluffy layouts.

Documents stay documents

CVs and letters always print on white with one pine accent, in a fixed ATS-safe template. The app's theme never touches them.

Colour

Use alias tokens only (surface, ink, action…), never raw hex. Every text pairing meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) and every control border meets 3:1, in both themes. Switch your system theme to see dark mode.

Space, shape and motion

A 4px base. Controls pad 8–12px, panels 20px, sections 56px apart. Pills for anything you press or that labels state; 10px for inputs and tables; 14px for panels; documents keep near-square corners.

Elevation and motion

Raised: a soft two-layer shadow in light mode; dark mode uses rules instead. Overlay: menus, dialogs, toasts.

Motion only answers your actions: 120ms for hovers, 200ms for opening and expanding, 320ms for page-level changes. Standard easing in, faster easing out. All motion respects reduced-motion.

Twelve core components, each shown in the states it can be in. Static examples of hover and focus are labelled; the live controls respond too.
