Design notes
  • Default sort is fit; the grey line under each title says why it ranks there, so the order is never a black box.
  • Requirement chips: green = you meet it, amber = partly or "preferred", red = a gap. Dealbreakers never appear here; those jobs are hidden with a reason.
  • "Sources" counts the postings merged into this job. Row opens the Job; checkbox enables bulk save/pass.
  • Source health is always visible in the top bar, so you never wonder whether the feed is complete.
Design notes
  • Over 10 seconds, so: determinate progress, a time estimate, and results streaming in as each source finishes.
  • Skeleton rows match the real row shape to avoid layout jumps.
Design notes
  • Never a blank table: the empty state says what will appear and gives two ways to start, plus the one-job shortcut.
Design notes
  • The old app closed every job of a failed source. Here a failed fetch changes nothing: jobs stay as they were, and the banner says so.
  • Health in the top bar turns red too. "Try now" retries just that source.
Design notes
  • The "a bug hid half my jobs" guard: a sharp drop against a source's normal volume is treated as suspicious, not as truth.
  • You decide whether it's real; until then nothing is closed.
Design notes
  • A filtered-to-zero view explains which filter is responsible and offers the one-click fix, instead of "No results".
Design notes
  • "Nothing disappears silently": every hidden job keeps a plain-language reason and can be unhidden.
  • "Not a dealbreaker for me" updates the rule, so the same mistake doesn't repeat.
Design notes
  • Long titles and company names truncate with the full text on hover; the why-line wraps.
  • Paging at 25 keeps the table scannable; fit order means the important rows are always on page one.
